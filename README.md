Kalshi Weather Market Backtesting & Analytics

Developed a Python-based backtesting and data analysis system to evaluate historical weather prediction markets on Kalshi across Miami, Phoenix, Atlanta, and Seattle.

The system retrieves historical market data through Kalshi's public API, analyzes market settlement outcomes, and evaluates threshold-based trading strategies. It also incorporates archived Open-Meteo weather forecasts to investigate whether forecast information can improve strategy selection.

Key features:
- Automated retrieval of historical Kalshi market data
- Analysis of weather-market settlement probabilities
- Historical simulation of NO-contract trading strategies
- Comparison of strategy performance across four cities
- Evaluation of forecast-based entry filters
- Excel dashboards displaying win rates, profitability, and research findings

Historical research results (January–September 2026):
- 442 simulated trades
- 392 wins and 50 losses
- 88.69% observed win rate
- $75.08 simulated gross profit before trading costs

Results represent historical simulations, not verified live trading returns. The project is designed for quantitative research and educational analysis.
